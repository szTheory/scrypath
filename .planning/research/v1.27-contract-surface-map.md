**Milestone:** v1.27 Adopter Contract Hardening

**Purpose:** Enumerate every adopter-facing or enforcement surface that must match the canonical install/support/proof contract.

## Canonical Contract Inputs

The following statements are the contract source for this milestone:

1. One canonical install/version statement across adopter-facing docs.
2. `main` may be unreleased; stable usage points to release-backed instructions.
3. `mix verify.adopter` is the canonical adopter proof spine.
4. Fast proof is routine; live proof is explicit and prerequisite-bound.
5. Adopter intake requires reproducible evidence and flow classification.
