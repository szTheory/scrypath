Instrument the Oban upsert and delete workers with stable telemetry spans and add the missing-record removal event.

Purpose: operators can observe every Oban upsert and delete job (OBS-01) and see when a job removes index entries whose records no longer exist (OBS-02), implementing CONTEXT decisions D-01 through D-13.
Output: `Scrypath.Telemetry.job_stop_metadata/1`, spans in both workers, the `[:scrypath, :oban, :upsert, :missing_removed]` point event, a reusable capture helper and scenario support module, and behavior tests.
</objective>
