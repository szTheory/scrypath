# Phase 157: Evidence-Based Performance — Retrospective Summary

> **Retrospective index (Phase 159, non-contemporaneous).** This record restores
> discoverability for Phase 157; it does not create a Phase 159 requirement or
> assert a contemporaneous performance run.

stable pure-path measurement reproducible and to accept optimization only with
evidence, rather than speculative tuning.

The canonical evidence and its limits are the [PERF-01 and PERF-02 rows in the
Phase 159 evidence matrix](../159-close-v1-37-audit-gaps-coverage-wiring-and-verification-prov/159-EVIDENCE-MATRIX.md#canonical-rows).

guarantee, or a claim of an accepted optimization.
