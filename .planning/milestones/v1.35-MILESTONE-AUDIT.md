v1.35 set out to turn a strategy-only brand prompt into a practical brand system: expert pressure-test, logo exploration, owner-selected identity, full transparent SVG family, portable tokens, subset fonts, standalone HTML brand book, examples, accessibility notes, and adoption across live repository surfaces.

Delivery matches that intent through direct evidence:

- The pressure-test, research, and decision log exist under `brandbook/notes/`.
- The final owner-selected logo direction is recorded as the copper `/` `scry/path` identity.
- The shipped brandbook contains assets, tokens, fonts, examples, and usage guidance.
- README, ScrypathOps, website, favicon, and OG surfaces adopted the new identity.
- The brand work did not add runtime search scope.

## Direct-Completion Finding

The milestone bypassed normal GSD plan execution. Phase 137 has a context file, but phases 138-143 do not have directories, PLAN files, SUMMARY files, validation files, or verification files.

This audit accepts the direct-completion evidence and closes the bookkeeping gap. Future agents should not recreate phase artifacts retroactively or reopen phases 137-143 because artifact-count tooling reports them as missing.
