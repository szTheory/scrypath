## Ordering Rationale

119–120 make the work visible and measurable; 121–123 are the compounding systemic fixes (highest
blast radius, so they carry the cross-AI gates); 124–126 are the per-screen dividends with the IA work
and the least-iterated Recover screens front-loaded for depth; 127 closes the loop. Gate phases:
121, 122, 123, 127 (plus cross-AI review on the 124 nav rename before commit).
