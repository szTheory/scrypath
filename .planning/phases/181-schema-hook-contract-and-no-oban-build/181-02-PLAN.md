<objective>
Declare the three duck-typed schema hooks as documented optional callbacks on `Scrypath.Schema`, inject the behaviour from `use Scrypath`, and flag wrong-arity hooks at compile time.

Purpose: HYG-01 and roadmap success criterion 1. Today Scrypath detects `search_document/1`, `search_document_id/1` and `search_records/2` with `function_exported?` only, so they are invisible in HexDocs and a wrong arity silently falls back to the default (research Pitfall 1: the compiler alone stays silent without `@impl`).
Output: callbacks with docs, `@before_compile` arity check, moduledoc Hooks section, tests, and the PROJECT.md HYG-01 claim.
</objective>
