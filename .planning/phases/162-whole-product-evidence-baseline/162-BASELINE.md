# Phase 162: Whole-Product Evidence Baseline

This is the single canonical claim-level evidence index for the approved non-UI readiness assessment. It links to evidence in its canonical location and does not copy earlier evidence matrices. This is an assessment artifact, not a readiness decision, defect list, or implementation plan. Phase 163 owns finding triage and disposition; Phase 164 owns the six-condition readiness gate in [the readiness program](../../reference/PRE-OPERATOR-UI-READINESS.md).

## Assessment contract

Dimensions are the program's seven approved dimensions: **1** public API consistency, ergonomics, compatibility, and error behavior; **2** core indexing and search correctness; **3** Ecto, Oban, Meilisearch, Phoenix, and packaged-consumer seams; **4** operational honesty, observability, backfill/reindex safety, failure reporting, and supportability; **5** first-hour and ongoing developer experience, docs, examples, diagnostics, and adopter issue intake; **6** security, privacy, dependency health, configuration boundaries, and release/supply-chain integrity; **7** architecture, readability, maintainability, measured performance, and test/CI signal-to-cost.

Roles use stable tokens: **integrator** installs/configures the library and gets a first indexed/search flow; **feature owner** chooses synchronization, maintains projection, and connects search to host context; **operator** configures credentials and diagnoses or recovers drift; **maintainer** keeps docs/support/version claims accurate and proves release integrity. Stages are `first-hour setup`, `indexing`, `search`, `failure diagnosis`, `recovery`, `upgrade`, and `release`.

Assessment vocabulary (D-06): `supported` means the linked evidence directly establishes this bounded claim; `insufficiently supported` means some relevant evidence exists but does not establish the claim; `unknown` means there is no adequate basis to decide. Freshness is independent: `current`, `reusable within its stated boundary`, `stale after a relevant invalidator`, or `unknown`. Age alone is not an invalidator. Revalidate only when a claim-specific code/test/config/docs, dependency/support, service/protocol, package/tag/release, workflow/security, incident, or time-sensitive external change bears on that row (D-07).

Dates are evidence dates unless explicitly labelled assessment date. Hosted receipts establish only their exact run, SHA, environment, and scenario. Phase 160's package consumer used Postgres 16 and Meilisearch v1.15 on candidate SHA `7931271abe53e83261d85a22077176f75898eb81`; its Phoenix lane is advisory (`continue-on-error: true`). The receipt does not cover document deletion, task listing, settings readback, index swap, facet search, or multi-search. Phase 161 release receipts cover Scrypath 0.3.13 and named release checks only. A selected compatibility tuple does not establish every supported-version cross-product. Host authorization, business policy, and presentation remain application-owned. Missing proof is not a defect. No score, blanket age cutoff, new required CI lane, broad rerun, or routine human UAT is introduced.
