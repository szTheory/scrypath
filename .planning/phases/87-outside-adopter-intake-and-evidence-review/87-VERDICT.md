# Final Verdict

Final verdict: related-data propagation

Decision gate: non-stop-soon requires Class A defended-path evidence

## Rationale
The evidence review produced two Class A submissions (Attempt 01 and Attempt 02), both demonstrating that integrating Scrypath on defended paths involves significant friction. 

Attempt 01 revealed a product gap regarding `related-data propagation` that currently requires a painful workaround and leaves a docs/onboarding gap. Attempt 02 revealed a product gap regarding `tenant-safe access` which causes a blocker and another painful workaround. 

While the frequency of each specific gap was only recorded as `one adopter`, the overarching theme of significant friction on Class A paths represents a repeated need for deeper integration features. Because `related-data propagation` is the locked number-one ranking for product work if the evidence justifies reopening, it is the winning evidence for the next phase. There were no higher-ranked alternatives available.

Phase 88 will focus on `related-data propagation` and clearing the bounded docs/support/env papercuts (minor docs/onboarding gaps) discovered in this phase.
