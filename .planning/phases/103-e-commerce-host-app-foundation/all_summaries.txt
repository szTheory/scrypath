## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Fixed ArgumentError generated by Oban jobs during seed run**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** Running `mix scrypath.seed` crashed Oban GenServer loops in the background because Ecto's `prepare_query/3` enforces `tenant_id` for all queries, but Oban does not provide a tenant or skip flag when querying its internal tables (`oban_peers` etc.).
- **Fix:** Modified `ScrypathEcommerce.Repo.prepare_query/3` to inspect the query source string. If the query is directed at an `oban_*` table, it skips the strict tenant enforcement check.
- **Files modified:** `examples/scrypath_ecommerce/lib/scrypath_ecommerce/repo.ex`
- **Commit:** ec86937
