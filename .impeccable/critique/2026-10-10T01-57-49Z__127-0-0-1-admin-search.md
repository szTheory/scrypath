## Persona red flags and cognitive load

- On-call engineer: pending-work detour and queue-clear implication are the primary confidence problems. Exact scoped URLs and the command palette are strengths.
- First-time Phoenix engineer: “failure class,” “row evidence,” “probe,” and “gated swap” add interpretation without helping the decision.

The loaded-file-to-preview gap forces users to remember identity rather than recognize it beside Run. The quiet health opening is reassuring; the pending-to-empty-work detour is the main emotional valley.
