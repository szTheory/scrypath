identity

The logo

The wordmark is the logo. Pristine Familjen Grotesk letters with one signature move: a weight-matched copper / path-separator. It reads instantly as a path, it's developer-native, and it never relies on a font at runtime (the SVG is outlined to vectors).

Clear space & minimum size

Keep clear space around the lockup equal to the cap-height of the “s” on all sides. Don't crowd it with other marks or text.

Minimum size. Wordmark never below 18px tall (≈96px wide). The s/p favicon is tuned to stay legible at 16px.

18px floor

Do / Don't

✓ do
  • Use the copper / at full strength — it's the signature.
  • Place on warm-neutral or night backgrounds with clear contrast.
  • Use the inverse file on dark; the monogram for avatars/favicons.
  • Let the wordmark breathe — honour the clear space.
✗ don't
  • No box, cage, pill, or filled background behind the mark.
  • Don't recolor the slash to a status color or gradient.
  • Don't stretch, condense, outline, or add shadow.
  • Don't add a tagline to the primary lockup (use the tagline file).

writing

Voice & microcopy

Calm, exact, capable. Explain — don't hype. Be specific. Assume engineering literacy. Sound composed under complexity. Metaphor sparingly.

✓ on-brand
  • “Reindexed 42,180 documents in 3.2s.”
  • “Add use Scrypath.Indexable to your schema.”
  • “Search across federated indexes with one query.”
✗ off-brand
  • “Blazing-fast, magical search! 🚀”
  • “The only search you'll ever need.”
  • “Effortlessly supercharge your data!”
