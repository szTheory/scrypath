Ecto-native · Elixir / Phoenix

Search that lives next to your data.

scrypath indexes straight from your Ecto schemas — no separate search cluster to babysit, no drift. Add one line, query everything.

Get started mix hex.info scrypath 
# lib/my_app/post.ex
use Scrypath.Indexable,
  fields: [:title, :body, :tags],
  adapter: Scrypath.Adapters.Postgres

# then, anywhere
Post |> Scrypath.search("ecto fragment") |> Repo.all()
/

One source of truth

Indexes derive from your schemas, so search can't drift from your data.

Composable queries

Federated fan-out
